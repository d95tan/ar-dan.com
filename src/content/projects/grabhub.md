GrabHub is designed to accommodate cyclists via a connection to the revamped rail corridor. This design is geared towards a new system of eScooter sharing by enhancing convenience for its users. The building acts as an accessible location for a multitude of services; F&B areas are specially designed to allow users to order food beforehand and pick it up as they head on to their next destination. The sporting goods store, bicycle shop and repair workshop are programmed adjacent to each other due to their synergistic nature, and the shower and parking space located in the same massing minimises unnecessary walking, while also serving as a waiting area for GrabFood riders.
