Situated on the edge of the cliff at Ubin Quarry, this eco-lodge is designed with the intention to create spaces that will bring people together.

With 3 group lodges that can accommodate up to 6 guests each, these lodges allow users to start their own campfires indoors with a breathtaking view of the night sky and the quarry. Extended columns in the lower section allow users to hang their hammocks in various configurations, creating a dynamic floor plan which adapts to the campers.

The 2 couple lodges create intimate spaces for couples to exist as either 2 independent entities or as a singular entity.
